About Sezhmon Terminal

Sezhmon Terminal is currently in its early development stages. Our goal is to build a reliable and functional Android CLI environment for enthusiasts and developers.

As this is a Pre-release version, we are constantly working to improve stability, performance, and features. We welcome your feedback to help us grow.

What to expect:

  • A foundational Command Line Interface for Android.
  • Ongoing improvements in speed and optimization.
  • A platform built on user feedback and testing.

Note: This is a pre-alpha/beta release. Features are subject to change.
